BY ALÉ

Jewelry should carry more than value.

It should carry a point of view.

Vida is built around the idea that the most meaningful jewelry does not begin with a trend or a catalog number. It begins with something worth remembering — then shape, stone, and gold are used to give that feeling a permanent form.

The collection stays intentionally small. Some pieces will exist once. Others may return in limited variations, but never at the expense of what made the original special.
