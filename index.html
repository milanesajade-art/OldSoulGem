MEET THE MAKER

A handmade brand rooted in creativity, intuition, and healing.

Old Soul Gem is Alejandra’s space for creating beauty with meaning.

What began as a love for crystals and handmade jewelry grew into a San Antonio small business centered on self-expression, thoughtful craftsmanship, and connection. Alejandra creates and curates pieces that celebrate natural color, individual energy, and the timeless feeling of finding something that simply feels like yours. Her work is also informed by her Reiki practice and a genuine desire to create a calm, welcoming experience for every customer.

Every stone has its own pattern, color, and character. That natural individuality is part of the beauty: the piece you choose is meant to feel personal, expressive, and uniquely yours.
