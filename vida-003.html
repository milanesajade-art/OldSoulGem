Luz de Alé

Private

A luminous oval opal surrounded by an asymmetrical burst of color, finished with flowing vine-like goldwork. The piece is designed to feel collected rather than manufactured — romantic, sculptural, and unmistakably individual.

Metal14K yellow gold
CenterWhite opal
AccentsPink, red, green, blue & champagne tones
EditionOne of One

Design story

Luz de Alé is built around light and movement: an opal center with color appearing around it like a small garden in motion. The goldwork stays organic and deliberately asymmetrical so the ring feels alive from every angle.
