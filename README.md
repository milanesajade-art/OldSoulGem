# Vida Collection by Alé

A private luxury jewelry collection and client experience platform.

## Vision

Vida Collection by Alé focuses on one-of-one and limited-production fine jewelry,
designed around distinctive gemstones, sculptural goldwork, and personal storytelling.

## Initial App Goals

- Showcase the jewelry collection
- Individual product pages
- One-of-One piece identification
- Gemstone and material details
- Pricing and availability
- Private client inquiries
- Future client accounts and custom-piece requests

## Brand

**Vida Collection by Alé**

Private fine jewelry centered on one-of-one and limited-production pieces.

**Brand direction:**
- Refined and understated luxury
- Sculptural, gemstone-forward designs
- 14K gold
- One-of-one pieces
- Personal storytelling behind each design
- Private, high-touch client experience

**Location:** San Antonio, Texas
