# Vida Collection by Alé — Collection Review

This file is the current review sheet for the first eight Vida directions. Anything marked **Locked** is treated as approved. Anything marked **Designer Review** is intentionally provisional and should be redlined before final production.

## Collection rules

- House brand: **Alé**
- First collection: **Vida Collection**
- Primary metal language: polished yellow gold
- Confirmed flagship metal: **14K yellow gold**
- Positioning: one-of-one and limited-production fine jewelry
- Design priority: gemstone-first, sculptural, story-led, never mass-market
- Ring forms should remain closely faithful to the approved references rather than being heavily reinterpreted
- Pricing marked **Private** is intentionally not finalized
